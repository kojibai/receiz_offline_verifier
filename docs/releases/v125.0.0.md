## The reality-grade stack

1. **Source.** Exact file, media, identity, action, or state bytes remain stronger than descriptions of them.
2. **Law.** Admission and transition rules are explicit, versioned, and independently executable. Self-hashable prose or metadata is not authorization.
3. **Sequence.** Causal order is carried by verified KKSv1.0 coordinates and exact prior heads. Chronos is a human-readable projection derived from Kai, never temporal authority.
4. **Object.** The enclosing sealed proof object binds identity, payload, provenance, custody, and history. Embedded hashes cannot outrank their enclosing artifact.
5. **Evidence.** Referenced proof objects are opened and verified before they can satisfy a requirement or support an inference.
6. **Reader.** UI, SDK, MCP, AI, database, registry, and institutional interpretation remain downstream projections.

## What v125 ships

### Temporal authority

- A Kai coordinate is accepted as authority only through the proof-bearing KKS/KaiSigil boundary; a decimal string is not enough.
- Live coordinates advance from a retained monotonic head, including multiple appends inside one millisecond and backward wall-clock movement.
- `createdAt` and every other Chronos field are descriptive bridges derived from Kai. They never order proof history.
- Legacy Chronos material remains available as a compatibility projection but cannot be promoted into authoritative KKS ordering.
- The public temporal-authority conformance suite freezes KKS basis, KaiSigil proof binding, strict monotonicity, and Chronos subordination.

### Identity, authorship, attestation, and credentials

- Every attestation event binds fresh Receiz ID/PBI authorship over its exact payload and prior head.
- Actor roles bind to an authorized domain/capability grantor; a subject cannot self-author a role and call it authorization.
- Referenced evidence proof objects must verify their subject, custody, content, and verifier bindings before they count.
- Application material cannot originate after application creation; supplemental evidence cannot move backward or arrive after its event.
- Evaluation, review, approval, issuance, renewal, replacement, suspension, and revocation obey exact causal coordinates and deadlines.
- Grant revocation is a coordinated append with an effective Kai/head, so valid historical events remain verifiable while later unauthorized use fails.
- Successor issuance binds the exact predecessor lifecycle append rather than only a credential identifier.

These are general primitives for any domain. They are not a custom authority for one verifier, institution, or use case.

### Local truth and continuous experience

- Known verified profile, Showcase, media, history, identity, wallet, market, and World truth paints immediately.
- A locally sealed append is visible immediately and remains the same object when global synchronization accepts it.
- Server synchronization appends verified additions; it does not replace, remount, or rehydrate already-held truth.
- Identity genesis is selected from the earliest verified Identity Record/Seal coordinate, never filenames or presentation claims.

- Creation Sigils preserve their original Kai pulse across legacy recovery, profile inspection, wallet surfaces, and World previews.
- Live-player and proof-media shells preserve visual continuity, including exact dark wide-screen backgrounds.

### Proof-native economy

- Settlement is spendable proof-native Phi. Reserve remains the named legacy funded rail.
- Existing eligible public sealed Showcase artifacts are recognized at already-accrued deterministic value. Activation is not a mint, bonus, multiplier, or backfill.
- Anyone can [recompute the exact value of any sealed Showcase proof object](v125.0.0-showcase-value-reproduction.md) at any admitted Kai moment from the verified object, verified coordinate, frozen projector digest, and integer rounding rule. No database or institutional price assertion is required.

- One artifact has one spendable membership. Custody moves that membership while provenance and witness history remain.
- Sender and receiver independently verify the exact sealed transition at their edges. Hosted infrastructure provides additive global synchronization and conflict serialization only.
