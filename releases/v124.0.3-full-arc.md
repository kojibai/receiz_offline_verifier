## V124.0.2 — Proof material became portable

V124.0.2 introduced the real playable-proof breakthrough. Small inline proof URLs could carry and play their exact verified material without network access. Larger media used bounded `rmc1` heads over exact committed segments because practical browser URL limits prevented carrying all bytes directly.

The mistake was not the playback primitive. Optional representation construction entered Record, Seal, Verify, and later export paths. Separately, Workbench rendered a PBI badge into exported pixels and used an exception that let the representation pass locally even though its native ownership basis no longer matched the current bytes.
