**first admission only, then append forever**.

- A sealed Original and accepted Identity Record are stronger than composer,
  session, upload, database, or projection state.
- `profile-showcase:<Receiz ID>` is a stable sealed composite proof identity.
- Every accepted profile state is a sealed successor carrying the exact
  predecessor, one bounded delta, admitted-child evidence, and carried history.

- Every accepted economy state is itself a sealed successor proof object.
- Market is an exact-digest derivative projection beneath the economy proof.
- Merkle roots, Fibonacci checkpoints, IndexedDB indexes, rows, manifests,
