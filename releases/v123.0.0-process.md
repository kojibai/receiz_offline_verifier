## Authority sequence

The object is authority at every stage. Edge verification precedes explicit consent; consent precedes narrow authority; SDK planning precedes execution; outcome lookup precedes ambiguous retry. Database and deployment status are evidence about availability and projection, not authority above the proof object.

## Manual boundaries

Publishing packages, applying migrations, deploying, signing attestations, tagging, and pushing are external mutations. They are not inferred from a request to prepare or commit the release and require their own authorization or operator action.
