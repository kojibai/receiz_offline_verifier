## Application migration

1. Continue verifying the enclosing proof object at the edge.
2. Replace application-made public world digests with `planCommandV122` and `planTransactionV122`.
3. Request Settlement or Reserve scopes through `scopesForRails`; inspect granted and missing scopes.
4. Exchange an identity artifact and explicit consent for narrow proof authority when an embedded flow must avoid a redirect.
5. Resolve subject namespaces at the exact head used for planning.
6. Execute the prepared Phi intent on its named rail.
7. Persist the exact intent and idempotency key.
8. On ambiguity, call `executionByIdempotencyKey` before retry.

Do not rename stable v122 protocol types. Do not treat a token or receipt as the identity artifact. Do not move USD. Do not merge Settlement and Reserve. Do not make database availability a prerequisite for edge-held truth.

The v123 SQL migration is required for production remote authority and Phi execution routes, but applying it is a separate production mutation requiring explicit authorization and evidence.
